Mean MAE is error over all holdout rows. All-zero mean MAE predicts zero everywhere on the same rows. MAE reduction is all-zero mean MAE minus mean MAE; positive is better. Improvement averages the per-seed percentage reductions.

Mean F1 averages each distinct seed's claim F1: 2 × precision × recall / (precision + recall), or 0 when both are zero. Higher is better (0–1); it measures claim detection, not amount accuracy.
